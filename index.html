justalida · at peace with the change

An Autumn Stroll

Watch the city pass you by.
Notice who calls out.

↓

ginkgo

Golden Leaves

Foreign friends forced
to call here their home.
Happy and bright, though,
as they stand together.

sunset

Purple-Pink Sky

Daylight fades
yielding to the in-between.
Is it not so lovely,
to stand on the threshold?

reflections

Allegheny Sparkles

The sisters of our city
twinkle each night,
not thinking of the water
dancing below.

arrival

The future holds?

read · wander · connect

enjoy the season, while she is with us
